Mission

Accountability should not be optional for the people with the most discretion.

Hold Prosecutors Accountable, Inc. is organized and operated exclusively for charitable and educational purposes under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. We promote fairness, accountability, transparency, and equal treatment in the criminal justice system.

We do that in three ways: we conduct and publish research on how criminal justice practices work in practice, we educate the public about prosecutorial authority and the rights of accused and formerly incarcerated people, and we advocate — within the limits that apply to charitable organizations — for laws and policies that make access to justice something other than a function of wealth, status, or institutional power.

Officials answer for misconduct
Government officials are appropriately accountable when they abuse the authority the public gave them.
A defense that is real
Accused people have meaningful access to a fair defense regardless of their financial resources.
Detention by judgment, not price
Pretrial detention decisions rest on individualized judicial determinations rather than an individual's ability to pay.
A sentence that ends
People who have completed their sentences have a meaningful opportunity to reintegrate into society.

Our work

Charging & discretion

Prosecutorial accountability

Prosecutors decide who is charged, what they are charged with, and what a plea will cost. Those decisions carry enormous weight and leave very little public record. Approach

Findings first, then the argument.

Research

We collect and analyze information on charging, plea, detention, and reentry practices, and we publish what we find so others can use it, check it, and build on it.

Public education

We explain how prosecutorial authority works, what rights accused and formerly incarcerated people have, and where the process quietly turns on money rather than judgment.

Policy advocacy

We advocate for specific, workable reforms, within the limits that apply to organizations exempt under Section 501(c)(3). We do not support or oppose candidates for public office.
